# RB-24 · Calling a classmate's API

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-22 · readers: everyone

## What you need from them

Their API URL, which ends in `execute-api.us-east-1.amazonaws.com`, and their `LAB_TOKEN`. Do not ask for their Gemini key; it stays in their function.

## From the terminal

`LAB_URL=https://THEIRS.execute-api.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/triage LAB_TOKEN=THEIR_TOKEN ./call.sh` sends one ticket to their function. Their function writes the row to their inference table and the line to their log, not yours.

## From the Swagger page

Open their URL with `/docs`, click Authorize, paste their token, and send a `POST /chat`.

## If theirs is down

Their Learner Lab session being off does not take the API down; the function answers without a session. If it is down, their function or key is broken, and the symptoms are the same as for your own: RB-05 for 401, RB-07 for 502 and 503.
