# RB-06 · The API answers 429

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-19 · readers: everyone

## What it means

A 429 from the API, with no `INFERENCE` line in the log, is the gateway's throttle. The function never ran. The throttle is set per stage with `./deploy.sh --throttle RATE BURST` and removed with `./deploy.sh --throttle off`.

## The lag

The setting takes tens of seconds to minutes to reach the edge in both directions, and is applied on a best-effort basis. A storm of requests right after `--throttle 2 5` is usually all 200; a storm right after `--throttle off` can still show some 429. Wait a minute and try again before concluding the setting did not take.

## A 429 with an INFERENCE line

Then the gateway let the request through and the model refused it. That is the provider's rate limit, RB-01, not the gateway's.
