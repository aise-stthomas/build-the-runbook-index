# RB-19 · The judge disagrees with you

owner: measurement team · as of 2026-10-22 · readers: everyone

## What the judge is

A second model call that reads an output and a rubric and answers yes or no. It is a model: it has an error rate, it is nondeterministic, and the vendor changes it.

## When you disagree with it

Label thirty outputs yourself, run the judge on the same thirty, and fill in the four counts: both yes, both no, judge yes and you no, judge no and you yes. The judge's sensitivity is the share of your "no" labels it also said no to; its specificity is the share of your "yes" labels it also said yes to. Report both with their counts. Then read every disagreement: judges fail in patterns, and the pattern is what to write into the rubric.

## When to do it again

When the judge's model changed, when the rubric changed, when the system under test started producing a different kind of text, and on a schedule regardless. A judge validated once, against a model that no longer exists, is an opinion.

## Its own noise

Run the judge three times on the same outputs. The share of verdicts that flip is the judge's noise floor, and no difference smaller than that between two versions of the system is a difference.
