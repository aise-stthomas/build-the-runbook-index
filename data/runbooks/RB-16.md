# RB-16 · Refund limits and who may approve

owner: support operations · as of 2026-10-21 · readers: everyone

## The limits

A refund of $50 or less is issued by the system itself. A refund above $50 and up to $200 is held for a human to approve. A refund above $200 is escalated to a team lead and is never issued by the system or by a front-line agent.

## Where the limit is enforced

In code, after the model. The model proposes an action and an amount; the validator in the triage function checks the amount against the limits and overrides the action if the model proposed a refund over the cap. A model that says "refund $88" produces a hold, not a refund, whatever its rationale says.

## Exception

Refunds for duplicate charges are issued in full at any amount, by the system, because the money was never the customer's to lose. The duplicate must be confirmed by two orders with the same amount within ten minutes on the same account; the validator checks this, not the model.

## Currency

Limits are in US dollars. An order in another currency is converted at the rate on the order, which is stored on the order row.
