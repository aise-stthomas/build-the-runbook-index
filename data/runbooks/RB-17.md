# RB-17 · Escalating a ticket to a person

owner: support operations · as of 2026-10-21 · readers: everyone

## When

Escalate when the model's action is `escalate`, when the validator held a refund above $200, when a customer asks for a person, or when a ticket mentions legal action, the press, or a safety issue.

## How

The escalation writes the ticket to the support queue with the triage record attached: the action proposed, the amount, the rationale, and the request id, so the person can read what the model saw and said. The customer gets an automatic reply saying a person will respond, and nothing else.

## Service level

A person responds within four business hours. Business hours are 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Central time, Monday to Friday.

## Exception

A ticket that reports a security problem, such as an account the customer did not open or a password they did not change, does not go to the support queue. It goes to the on-call engineer by page, at any hour, and the customer is told within thirty minutes.
