# RB-23 · The queue is backing up

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-22 · readers: everyone

## Symptom

Tickets wait more than fifteen minutes between arrival and triage. The queue depth climbs and does not come down.

## Why adding workers does not help

The consumer is bounded by the model's per-minute rate, not by compute. More consumer functions produce more 429s from the provider and the same throughput. The queue is the shock absorber: it holds work until the model can take it.

## What to do

First check whether the model is refusing (RB-01): a backlog that started at the same moment as a run of 429s is the provider's rate limit, and the fix is to wait or to use a key with a higher limit. Then check the consumer's reserved concurrency; a value of 1 serializes every ticket. Raise it to the number of requests per minute the key allows, divided by the per-ticket latency in minutes.

## During a vendor outage

Do not scale. Let the queue hold the tickets; set the customer-facing reply to "we have your ticket" and let the backlog drain when the model is back. Tickets older than four hours in the queue are escalated to the support queue by the age rule, so nobody waits forever.
