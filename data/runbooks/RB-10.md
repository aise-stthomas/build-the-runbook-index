# RB-10 · The Learner Lab session expired

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-20 · readers: everyone

## Symptom

Every AWS command fails with `ExpiredToken: The security token included in the request is expired`, or `deploy.sh` stops at `sts get-caller-identity failed`. A Learner Lab session lasts about four hours and its credentials die with it.

## Fix

Start the Learner Lab again from the course page, open AWS Details, copy the three credential lines, and paste them over the `[default]` section of `~/.aws/credentials`. Keep the `[default]` header: a file without it fails with `Unable to parse config file`.

## What survives a session ending

Everything deployed: the function, the HTTP API, the inference table, the bucket, the scheduled rule, and the log groups. They keep working and keep answering requests while the session is off. Only your laptop's credentials expire.

## What does not survive

An upload that was in progress. A `deploy.sh` that fails mid-upload with `Connection was closed` had its credentials cancelled under it; start a fresh session and run it again.
