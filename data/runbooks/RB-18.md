# RB-18 · A customer asks to delete their account

owner: support operations · as of 2026-10-21 · readers: everyone

## The rule

Account deletion is completed within 30 days of the request. The customer receives a confirmation when the request is logged and a second one when the deletion is done.

## What is deleted

The account row, the order history, the ticket text, and the customer's rows in the inference table. The inference table rows are found by the account id in the row, so the deletion job has to read the table; the row's request id is kept in an anonymized form for the audit record.

## Exception

An account with an open refund, a hold awaiting approval, or an escalated ticket cannot be deleted until those are settled. The deletion request is logged, the customer is told why it is waiting, and the 30 days count from the request, not from the settlement.

## Who may do it

Only support operations runs the deletion job. Front-line agents log the request and tell the customer.
