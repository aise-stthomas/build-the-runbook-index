# RB-01 · The model answers 429

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-19 · readers: everyone

## Symptom

A call to the model fails with HTTP 429 and the message "You exceeded your current quota, please check your plan and billing details". The message is the same whether the per-minute limit or the daily cap was hit, so the words alone do not tell you which one it is.

## Tell the two apart

Read the structured error details, not the message. A `QuotaFailure` entry names the quota that ran out in its `quotaId`. If the id contains `PerMinute`, the minute is not over yet. If it contains `PerDay`, the key's daily allowance is used up and no amount of waiting will help before midnight Pacific time. A `RetryInfo` entry carries a `retryDelay` such as `17s`: that is how long the provider asks you to wait.

## Per-minute limit

Wait as long as `retryDelay` says, plus one second, and send the request again. The retry helper in `src/system/plumbing.py` does this for you and gives up after fifteen minutes of continuous rate limiting, because at that point something other than the per-minute limit is wrong.

## Daily cap

Stop the run. Everything recorded so far is kept. Rerun the same command tomorrow, or with a partner's key, and it continues where it stopped. Do not create a second key on the same account to get around the cap: the cap is per project, and a second key shares it.
