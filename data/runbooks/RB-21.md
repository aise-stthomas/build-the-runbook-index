# RB-21 · Cost and the Learner Lab budget

owner: platform team · as of 2026-10-22 · readers: everyone

## The budget

The Learner Lab has a fixed $50 budget for the semester and no card behind it. When it is spent, the account stops. A usage alarm at $10 and another at $25 were set on the first day; they send mail to the address on the account.

## What costs nothing while idle

A function, an HTTP API, a DynamoDB table on on-demand pricing, an EventBridge rule, a log group, and a bucket with a few megabytes in it. Leaving the triage service deployed between labs costs nothing measurable.

## What does cost

Invocations, in tiny amounts; a scheduled rule firing every five minutes for a week, in slightly less tiny amounts; and anything that is not serverless. Do not create EC2 instances, NAT gateways, load balancers or Kubernetes clusters: they are not allowed under the lab role, and one of them running for a weekend would end the budget.

## If the alarm fires

Find what is running with `aws lambda list-functions`, `aws events list-rules` and the billing console, and disable the schedule with `./schedule.sh --off`.
